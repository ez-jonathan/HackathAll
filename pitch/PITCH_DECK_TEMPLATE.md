# 📊 5-Slide Hackathon Pitch Deck Framework

> **Tip**: Keep text sparse (under 25 words per slide). Use bold numbers, clear screenshots, and sharp contrasts.

---

### Slide 1: The Hook & The Problem
- **Headline**: The [X-billion dollar / high friction] Problem
- **Body**:
  - The status quo: How users currently suffer.
  - The real cost: Lost time, lost money, cognitive overhead.
- **Visual**: A visceral statistic or screenshot of the painful broken workflow.

---

### Slide 2: The Solution ([Project Name])
- **Headline**: 10x Faster [Core Function]
- **Body**:
  - The 1-sentence value proposition.
  - The 3 unfair advantages of your approach.
- **Visual**: Hero screenshot of your app UI in dark mode.

---

### Slide 3: How It Works (The Tech Edge)
- **Headline**: Architecture Built for Speed & Reliability
- **Body**:
  - Deep integration with Sponsor APIs / LLMs / Agent loops.
  - Resilient design: Instant streaming, offline mock fallback, zero data loss.
- **Visual**: Clean, modern architecture diagram (Mermaid flow).

---

### Slide 4: Market Validation & Business Model
- **Headline**: Who Pays & Why Now?
- **Body**:
  - Target beachhead market: Who buys this first?
  - Unit economics / Monetization strategy (SaaS, usage-based, API tier).
- **Visual**: TAM/SAM/SOM breakdown or 3 targeted enterprise personas.

---

### Slide 5: The Team & Vision
- **Headline**: Built in 24 Hours. Scaling Tomorrow.
- **Body**:
  - Core team strengths (Full-stack, AI, Product).
  - Next 30 days roadmap: Beta launch, user testing, sponsor integration depth.
- **Links**: Live Demo URL | GitHub Repository | Pitch Video
